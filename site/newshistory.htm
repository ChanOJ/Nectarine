[22/08/2006]  modules.pl - now in english! (posted by Aceman)
Hi sceners! After 2 years of existence, modules.pl is finally avaible in english version, so every non-polish visitors can now easily browse it .

What is modules.pl? It's a site devoted only to tracked music, gathering musicians and module lovers from all over the world . It's main purpose is to collect the best modules ever made and create place, where every tracker musician could easily insert his/her tunes.

Okay, nothing more to say.. enjoy!
